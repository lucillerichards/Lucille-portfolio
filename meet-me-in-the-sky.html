ALBUM ARTWORK · NORTHERN LIGHTS A CAPPELLA

MEET ME IN THE SKY

Meet Me In the Sky was Northern Lights’ first full album, featuring arrangements from across multiple generations and reflecting years of planning, personal changes and creative passion. As such, it felt impossible to ignore the need to incorporate the group’s logo and I strove to create an album cover which would act as the centrepiece of this creative era of Northern Lights. That was in the earliest stages of ideation and while other album details were still falling into place.

Together with the wider creative team, we made decisions about the album’s title, looking through the lyrics within our repertoire for inspiration for something which resonated with the significance of the album’s release, as well as the unprecedented successes we had achieved within that year. As it happened, we found that within a song not featured on the album, but one which we considered particularly special for the magic we experienced when it was performed - Bebe Rexha and martin Garrix’s In the Name of Love. The line 'would you rise up, come and meet me in the sky?' encapsulated the song’s message: what you would do for love. It also linked thematically with many tracks on the album: 'What I Did For Love' and 'Where Is Love?', for example.

Once we had settled on 'Meet Me In The Sky', I looked into imagery which would reflect ideas discussed in the naming process. The sky and stars came easily to depict magic and an ethereal quality - it also happened to fit well with long established aurora borealis imagery within the group, through the name.
